# Illustrated learning sources

The new studies use the existing embedded KJV corpus for every Scripture excerpt. Artwork, editorial explanations, and review answers are reading aids, kept outside the Scripture containers and speech selection. These are artistic reconstructions, not archaeological findings or engineering drawings.

## Inside Noah’s Ark

- **The three decks:** Genesis 6:14–16. Rooms, pitch inside and outside, dimensions, and three stories are specified. The cutaway floor plan and detailed joinery are illustrative.
- **The door in the side:** Genesis 6:16; 7:16. The location and later shutting-in are in the text. Door construction, frame, and other visual details are not specified.
- **The window:** Genesis 6:16; 8:6–9. The opening and the sending of the raven and dove are in the text. The pictured window/shutter is a visual interpretation.
- **Food for the journey:** Genesis 6:19–22. Food is gathered for Noah and the living creatures. Particular foods, sacks, and baskets are illustrative choices.

Reference reading: [Genesis 6:14–21, KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%206%3A14-21&version=KJV), [Genesis 8:6–9, KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%208%3A6-9&version=KJV).

## The Sower’s Field

- **Wayside:** Matthew 13:3–4, 18–19.
- **Stony ground:** Matthew 13:5–6, 20–21.
- **Thorns:** Matthew 13:7, 22.
- **Good ground:** Matthew 13:8–9, 23.

Each stop pairs the parable with its explanation in the same chapter. Editorial summaries do not add a new interpretation beyond those paired passages. The four-part landscape is an illustrative arrangement, not a map of a documented field; the plant varieties and exposed soil profile are visual aids.

Reference reading: [Matthew 13, KJV](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%2013&version=KJV).

## Artwork and speech

The two overview scenes and eight detail illustrations were generated individually with built-in ImageGen, using the existing Tabernacle art as a style reference. No close-up was cropped from a scene. Unchanged source PNGs are retained in `assets/imagegen/source/learn/`; production WebPs are in `web/art/learn/`. Exact prompts, source output identifiers, dimensions, processing, and hashes are in [the learning provenance](../assets/imagegen/learn/provenance.json) and [the asset manifest](../assets/imagegen/manifest.json).

The social preview is a full-frame JPEG format derivative of the previously approved Tabernacle image. Sharing titles and descriptions are supplied by HTML metadata. A metadata image is not evidence that a third-party service has refreshed its cached preview.

Only KJV verse containers are included in read-aloud. Scene descriptions, reconstruction notes, questions, answers, navigation, and artwork captions are excluded.
