# Exodus chapter companions

All 40 chapters have source-linked editorial diagrams. Their 281 nodes partition all 1,213 KJV verses into reading stages. These diagrams explain sequence, instructions, lists, and relationships; they are not geographical maps or simultaneous narrative scenes. The focused catalog takes precedence only within each scene’s authored source ranges. Scripture and narration continue to use the embedded KJV text.

The native diagrams rebuild with `python3 scripts/build-exodus-companions.py`. Artwork originals, prompts, and conversion/inspection provenance are retained under `assets/imagegen/`; diagram provenance is under `assets/diagrams/`.

## Additional focused paintings

| Scene | Authored ranges | Hotspots |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Hail and fire strike the land of Egypt | Exodus 9:23–25 | 2 |
| Locusts cover the land and consume what the hail left | Exodus 10:14–15 | 2 |
| The people gather manna after the dew lifts | Exodus 16:14–18 | 2 |
| Moses strikes the rock and water comes out | Exodus 17:6 | 1 |
| The golden calf and the gathering | Exodus 32:4–6 | 2 |
| The cloud covers the Tabernacle | Exodus 40:34–35 | 2 |
| Israel departs from Egypt | Exodus 12:34,37–38 | 2 |
| Passover instructions for each household | Exodus 12:7–11 | 2 |
| The people meet at Mount Sinai amid cloud, smoke, fire, and lightning | Exodus 19:16–18 | 2 |

Source timing is explicit: the water-from-rock painting starts at 17:6, following the command at 17:5; the Sinai painting ends at 19:18, before the audible exchange in 19:19. At 40:35 the active subject becomes Moses outside, rather than being masked by the preceding cloud subject. Passover imagery illustrates instructions in 12:7–11. It is not proof of a specific household’s historical appearance.

Existing object studies remain available in Illustrations. Exodus 28, 29, and 31 verses without a matched object open an unselected Tabernacle overview. Fourteen chapter-diagram nodes also offer relevant existing object detail artwork.
