# Genesis Journey selected-episode coverage

This source-level ledger records which Genesis passages have a selected Journey episode or an exact legacy Eden stop anchor, and gives the exact remaining verse intervals for every chapter. It does not claim whole-chapter illustration or live runtime acceptance.

The consolidated catalog’s top-level `ranges` decide when a scene is selected. Each hotspot’s active/source ranges and anchor are listed separately. A verse can therefore belong to a selected scene while lacking its own hotspot; those cases are flagged below. The legacy Eden renderer has broad contextual routing ranges, but those are not counted as episode coverage. The flood Journey covers Genesis 7–8; the separate bow artwork covers Genesis 9:12–17, while Genesis 9:1–11 remains unillustrated at episode level.

The remaining intervals are computed from the local King James Version verse counts. “Unillustrated” here means no source-linked episode interval or exact legacy Eden stop anchor is assigned; it does not mean the reader shows no contextual backdrop.
