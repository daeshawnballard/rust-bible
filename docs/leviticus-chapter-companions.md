# Leviticus chapter companions

The 27 chapter diagrams contain 169 editorial units that partition all 859 KJV verses. They explain offerings, ritual classifications, priestly duties, holy days, social responsibilities, and land/household cycles. Instructions, narrative events, and lists remain labeled separately; ritual impurity is not treated as a modern medical diagnosis. Scripture remains in the reader and narration.

Rebuild diagrams with `python3 scripts/build-leviticus-companions.py`. The checked-in catalog retains sources, ranges, anchors, complete editorial explanations, and concise card summaries. The original ImageGen PNGs and exact prompts are retained in `assets/imagegen/`; the web-facing files are WebP.

## Focused illustrations

| Passage | Subject | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2:1 | Fine flour, oil, frankincense | Three distinct inspectable objects share one verse. The altar is contextual; the painting does not depict the later ritual in verses 2–3. |
| 8:7–9 | Robe, breastplate, turban | Garment types are named by the passage. Faces, colors, fastenings and other details are illustrative. Urim and Thummim are not presented as visible identified objects. |
| 16:20–22 | The live goat | Existing illustration retained. |
| 23:40;42–43 | Gathered branches and temporary booths | Illustration of instructions. Verse 41 remains with the calendar diagram; the scene does not identify a documented settlement. |

Journey prioritizes a focused scene only inside its authored intervals, then uses the chapter diagram. Diagram controls occupy a dedicated gutter, with camera focus on the readable card. Inspecting a subject preserves the reading/audio position; its explicit Read source link changes that position.

## Verification boundary

Source partitions, tests, artifact rebuilds, local browser flows, and public release checks are recorded separately. Full chapter-companion coverage does not mean that every verse is painted. This document is an implementation register, not deployment proof.
