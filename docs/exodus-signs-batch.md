## Timing and visual meaning

The rods diagram separates Aaron’s action, the magicians’ imitation, and the swallowing. Automatic follow selects the imitation at7:11 and the swallowing at7:12, even though its supporting source range includes both verses. The river caption labels reconstructed figures and setting; its rod-contact and changed-water anchors were chosen after inspecting the actual final painting.

The frogs painting connects the announced kneading-trough detail at8:3 with the fulfillment across the land at8:6. Its caption explicitly calls it an editorial composition. It does not place Aaron in a household. The lice diagram separates the command, result, failed imitation, and recorded speech; no physical hand or exact species is claimed. The flies comparison labels Goshen’s distinction as an announcement at8:22–23, alongside the reported swarm at8:24, without inventing a map.

Pins are anchored to the visible rod meeting the water, foreground river, kneading trough, frogs on the ground, or an explicit source-diagram node. Incidental frogs remain in the illustration’s bottom band after two generation attempts; the selected subjects are above the player. Exact crop and marker access must be checked through the actual responsive reader.
